whoami

I'm a second-year CS student who got hooked the first time I watched one of my own XSS payloads fire in a browser. Since then I've been training on TryHackMe, solving picoCTF challenges, and working through the PortSwigger Web Security Academy — and building my own deliberately vulnerable apps to understand flaws from the inside out.

My strongest languages are Python, C and C++. I can comfortably read and work with the MERN stack — I'm not yet shipping full MERN apps solo, and I'd rather say that plainly than oversell it.

So far I've shipped an XSS-vulnerable study lab, a CTF crypto & stego toolkit, and made my first open-source contribution. Next up: deeper AppSec skills and a summer internship with a real security team — if that's you, I'd love to talk.
